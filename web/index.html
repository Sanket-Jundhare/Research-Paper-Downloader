Systematic review toolkit

Download free research papers in one click.

Add your Excel or CSV paper list and run. PaperFetch finds and downloads all legally open-access PDFs, counts Total, Downloaded, and Not Found, and exports a detailed Excel report explaining why any missing paper could not be downloaded.

What PaperFetch outputs

  • All Free OA PDFs (ZIP)

    Validated real PDFs sorted into publisher folders (IEEE, Springer, Nature, Elsevier, PubMed, MDPI, ACM, Wiley).

  • Live Counts

    Instant counters for Total, Downloaded, and Not Found with exact breakdown.

  • Excel Report (.xlsx)

    Multi-sheet workbook: Summary, All Papers, Downloaded, and Not Downloaded with the exact Reason Why Not & clickable links.
